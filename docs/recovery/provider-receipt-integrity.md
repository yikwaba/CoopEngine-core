# Provider receipt integrity and reversal boundary

Generic journal reversal now refuses any provider-linked domain journal, PAYMENT_UNALLOCATED cash receipt, PAYMENT_ALLOCATED suspense release, or provider_transaction source. A ledger-only reversal cannot undo member savings/shares/loan projections, intent progress and allocation state. No provider refund or coordinated domain reversal is implemented here; these remain separate recovery gates. Legitimate manual journal and coordinated payroll reversal stay on their existing paths. The provider-link lookup takes no provider-row lock after locking the journal, preserving the allocation lock order.

Staff Collections and own-member receipt reads collapse exact delivery aliases into one canonical bank receipt. Correlation requires same cooperative, MONNIFY transaction reference, exact amount and virtual account. The original oldest notification is canonical, and deliveryCount is visible. Staff also receive every delivery reference. Member reads group only that member's original receipts and reveal no destination member or staff-only reference list. Missing or contradictory identities remain separate and review-required. Grouped staff totals, filtering and pagination agree. Notification rows, raw payloads, ownership and timestamps are retained; no migration or historical monetary rewrite occurs.

PostgreSQL acceptance covers refusal of every parked/allocation leg, direct savings/share/loan matches, callback/allocation/reversal races, alias grouping and pagination, inconsistent identities, tenant boundaries, redirected receipt privacy and historical reversed-journal review. Existing manual/payroll reversal regression tests remain active. Isolated browser acceptance covers staff/member delivery counts, one visible receipt, refused generic reversal before and after allocation, lost-response original recovery, duplicate callback, exact balance/progress, reconciliation, mobile and reload.
