# Member withdrawal approval identity — REC-08 / REC-13

Member self-service withdrawal requests now use active WITHDRAWAL policies with the same ordered staff approval engine as staff requests. The authenticated member principal supplies the requester identity; the public DTO does not accept a requester member ID. No member is represented by a fabricated staff user.

Append-only migration 0046 keeps historical staff requested_by values, adds requested_by_member_id and SUBMIT actor_member_id, and enforces exactly one request identity. Composite member foreign keys bind the member to the request/action tenant. Member origins are restricted to withdrawal requests and submission actions. Member deletion with referenced approval history is refused. Existing RLS policies remain in force; the new columns inherit them.

The owning transaction checks that the member identity matches the pending member withdrawal before selecting exactly one active amount policy and snapshotting its steps. Gaps, overlaps and missing steps roll back the parked request, submission and financial receipt. Request and final-decision retries retain the existing receipt protocol and exact decimal amounts. Staff decisions remain authorized through staff guards; a member session cannot approve. With no active policy, member requests stay parked on the existing legacy staff approval path. Previously parked requests and committed receipt responses are not retroactively changed.

The staff inbox labels member requesters and returns their member ID separately from the nullable staff requester ID. Six real-PostgreSQL cases cover origin/trail, ordered decisions, concurrent retries and one payout, rejection, policy failures, own-account and tenant isolation, legacy compatibility and database identity constraints. A separate isolated browser script uses actual member OTP sign-in and its cookie session to submit/retry the API request, then actual treasurer/chairman inbox and withdrawal screens to decide it. The existing 12 browser scripts remain.

Open: dedicated member withdrawal form and richer status history, policy administration, concurrent policy edits, historical bypass review, independent finance acceptance and production verification. Staff and member identities are separate account domains; no verified staff-to-member identity link exists, so dual-account natural-person segregation cannot be inferred from matching names or email addresses. No production merge or deployment is authorized by this slice.
